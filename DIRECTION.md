# DeskGlass direction (Jon's standing requirements)

Later rounds must follow these.

1. **WorldBox-style autonomous god-sim village. NO player god powers.** The player does not trigger powers or tap the map to act; the world runs itself and Jon just watches (he can restart the world any time with Start over, and does so if everyone dies, so there is NO need for a win condition or auto-restart). Several races/kingdoms with coloured banners and drawn borders, cities that grow, war/alliance/trade/rebellion, multi-day world ages (Hope, Sun, Moon, Wonders, Dark, Tears, Chaos, Ice, Ash, Despair) that change the mood, weather and economy. Dangers happen on their own: wars between kingdoms, and PREDATORS (wolf packs and bears hunt villagers away from the fire, fighters defend, others flee, kills give meat). Natural disasters may fire on their own from world ages, never from player taps. Build on what exists; never redo it.
2. **Better graphics every round.** Richer pixel art in `ui/CivArt.kt` (race sprites, per-faction buildings, animals, bridges, walls, ships, tiles, animated water/fire, particles, lighting) and smoother rendering. Gemini only if `GEMINI_API_KEY` already exists in the environment; never ask for a key, never commit one. No Godot conversion, no new setup for Jon, keep bundled assets small.
3. **UI polish every round**, on phones and tablets of all sizes (spacing, typography, glass effects, motion, tap feedback, empty states).
4. **Villager speech is grounded in real state.** Prompts carry only real facts (needs, stock, weather/season/time, nearby people and what they said, family, jobs, factions, events, chronicle, memories) and tell the model never to invent buildings, people, resources or events. Rule-based fallback lines are built from real facts too. Conversations are two-way: replies answer what was said and both sides remember it. Stories and gossip cite real chronicle/log events. Children speak simply.
5. **Dialogue must be robust.** Tolerant JSON repair, action validation, one retry with a shorter prompt, a request watchdog so a slow model never freezes the sim, near-duplicate suppression, length caps, think-tag/markdown stripping, a visible brain status, and a throttled Error log.
6. **Villagers use only the local model** (Ollama over Tailscale), with an offline rule fallback. No Claude-API brain for villagers.
7. **The app self-updates** from GitHub Releases. CI builds on every push to `main`; never leave `main` broken. Old saves must keep loading.
8. **The world must feel alive, not like pixels walking over a dead map.** Everything on screen should move or react: villagers face where they walk and talk, lean into steps, breathe, carry what they gather, show emotes, kick up dust and throw chips when they work; crops grow with the farm; grass, reeds and trees sway; water has foam, lilies, ducks and jumping fish; rabbits hop, dragonflies dart; rain splashes; chimneys smoke; things cast shadows that follow the sun; night is real darkness lit only by fires and windows. Ground is one painted image (`GroundGen` in `ui/PixelFx.kt`) with curved coasts and ragged biome borders, and sprites are polished to twice their grid size with outlines (`PixelFx.polish`). Keep adding life each round; pure drawing helpers go in `PixelFx.kt` so they can be tested on a plain JVM.
